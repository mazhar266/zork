ZORK I: The Great Underground Empire - complete playthrough
=============================================================
Played start to finish through the zork MCP server (start_game / send_command /
save_game / load_game) by Claude Sonnet 5.5.

Result: WON. Score 350 of 350, rank Master Adventurer, in 539 moves.

How to read this file:
  - Lines starting with ">" are the player's input; everything else is the
    game's own reply, copied from the interpreter.
  - The player saved often. When a move ended badly (a death, a lost fight, a
    sword knocked away), the game was reloaded from a save and played on. The
    abandoned attempts (6 of them, 54 moves in total) are left out
    so the text below is the single line of play that actually reached the end.
    Save and load commands themselves are not shown either.
  - The status line the interpreter prints (room / score / moves) is omitted.
  - "<start>" marks the opening text printed when the game starts.
